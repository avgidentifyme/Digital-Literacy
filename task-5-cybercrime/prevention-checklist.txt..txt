Stay Safe Online — Checklist for College Students (India)

 Use strong, unique passwords--for each account (mix letters, numbers, symbols) and avoid using your name or DOB.
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)-- on email, social media, and banking apps for extra security.
Do not click on unknown links--in emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages—even if they look urgent or official.
Verify before sharing personal information--like OTPs, passwords, or ID details—legitimate organizations never ask for these.
Keep your devices updated** (phone, laptop, apps) to protect against security vulnerabilities.
Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities** like banking or logging into important accounts.
Download apps only from trusted sources** like the Google Play Store or Apple App Store—avoid third-party APKs.
Check privacy settings on social media** and avoid oversharing personal details like your location, college ID, or routine.

Financial / UPI Safety Tips:

Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money—PIN is only required for sending money.
Always verify payment requests before approving—fraudsters often send “collect requests” pretending to pay you.

If you become a victim of cybercrime:

 Report immediately on the National Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in)
 Call the Cyber Crime Helpline **1930** for quick assistance and to try to block fraudulent transactions.

Staying alert and informed is your best defense against cyber threats.
